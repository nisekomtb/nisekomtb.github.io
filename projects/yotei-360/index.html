Planning

Niseko's Future Signature Ride

Every legendary mountain bike destination has a signature ride: the Whole Enchilada in Moab, Peak to Creek in Whistler. Yotei 360 will be Niseko's. A 50km+ loop circling the base of Mt Yotei, one of Japan's most iconic volcanic peaks, combining existing gravel roads with purpose-built trail. An alpine odyssey through forests, farmland, and mountain landscapes.

What's Planned

  • ✓ 50km+ circumnavigation route around Mt Yotei
  • ✓ Mix of existing gravel roads and purpose-built singletrack
  • ✓ Forest, farmland, and alpine terrain
  • ✓ Connections to the wider Niseko trail network
