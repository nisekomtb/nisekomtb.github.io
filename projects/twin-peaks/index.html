Japan's Largest Free-Access MTB Network

Twin Peaks is NAMBA's flagship project — Japan's largest free-access, nonprofit-built mountain bike trail network. Over 21 kilometres of Swiss-designed trails spanning 20+ routes, from beginner-friendly flow to expert technical terrain. Open every day of the riding season, free to all, no registration required.

Growth Timeline

2022

Construction begins with Allegra Tourismus. First 4.7km of trail built.

2023

Park opens to the public. 14.2km of trail, first community events.

2024

7,750+ visitors. Dirty Dames women's programme launches. 20+ trails. Loic Bruni visits.

2025

11,500+ visitors. Major expansion. Hirafu connection. Pinkbike Trail of the Month.

2026

Green Flow Trail and Lower Green Climb pending sponsorship. The network continues to grow.

The Allegra Partnership

Every trail at Twin Peaks is designed and built in collaboration with Allegra Tourismus, a world-renowned Swiss trail building company bringing decades of Alpine expertise to Niseko's volcanic landscape.

100% Community-Funded

Twin Peaks is 100% community-funded. 93% private funding, 69% from local businesses, 92% goes directly to trail building. Every trail has been fully sponsored.
