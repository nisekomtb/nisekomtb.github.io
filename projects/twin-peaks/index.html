Operational

Japan's Largest Free-to-Ride MTB Park

Twin Peaks is NAMBA's flagship project: Japan's largest free-to-ride mountain bike park. Over 17km of Swiss-designed trails across 20+ routes, from beginner flow lines to expert technical terrain. Open every day of the riding season. No fees, no registration.

Growth Timeline

2022
4.4km · NPO formed

NPO officially registered. Kutchan town approves trail-building rights at Twin Peaks. 4.4km built in a COVID-shortened window, under a 1m width restriction, and without cutting down a single tree.

2023
Twin Peaks opens · ¥29M raised

Twin Peaks opens to the public. 6km built in one summer, the fastest project Allegra had ever seen. Network reaches 10.4km. Hokkaido's first Soil Searching event.

2024
7,750 visitors · Taki Tech opens

3.4km of new trail at Twin Peaks, including Taki Tech, our first double-black. 7,750 visitors. Dirty Dames launches with 30+ women at the opening ride. Loic Bruni stops by.

2025
11,500 visitors · jump line opens

Visitors up 48% to 11,500. Twin Peaks' first jump line opens, paired with a dedicated climb trail for hot laps.

2026
Green Flow and Lower Green Climb

Green Flow Trail and Lower Green Climb pending sponsorship. The network keeps growing.

The Allegra Partnership

Every trail at Twin Peaks is designed and built with Allegra, the Swiss firm behind some of the best-known trail networks in the Alps and beyond. The 2023 build of 6km in one summer was the fastest project Allegra had ever delivered.

100% Community-Funded

Twin Peaks is 100% community-funded. Across 2022 to 2025, 92% of funding came from private and community sources, with 74% from local businesses. 82% of every yen goes directly to trail construction; 95% to trails counting maintenance. Every trail has been fully sponsored.
