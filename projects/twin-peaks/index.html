Operational

Japan's Largest Free-to-Ride MTB Park

Twin Peaks is NAMBA's flagship project: Japan's largest free-to-ride mountain bike park. Over 17km of Swiss-designed trails across 20+ routes, from beginner flow lines to expert technical terrain. Open every day of the riding season. No fees, no registration.

Growth Timeline

2022
4.4km · park begins

Kutchan town grants permission to rent land for the Twin Peaks Bike Park. With a COVID-shortened build window, we still finish 4.4km of trail, under a 1m width restriction, without cutting down a single tree.

2023
10.4km · park opens

We gamble on an aggressive build window and plan an additional 6km of trail. The build completes on time, the fastest project Allegra had ever delivered. Twin Peaks opens to the public in September.

2024
13.8km · first double-black

Taki Trail and Taki Tech bring the region its first double-black. The park grows to 13.8km.

2025
17.8km · jump line opens

Niseko's first jump line opens, paired with a dedicated climb trail for hot laps. Network reaches 17.8km. Work begins on the Lower West Climb.

2026
Lower West Climb · first green downhill

Lower West Climb finishes, connecting to last year's jump line climb. The region's first green downhill trail joins the Lower Green Climb. Both trails are still available to sponsor.

The Allegra Partnership

Every trail at Twin Peaks is designed and built with Allegra, the Swiss firm behind some of the best-known trail networks in the Alps and beyond. The 2023 build of 6km in one summer was the fastest project Allegra had ever delivered.

100% Community-Funded

Twin Peaks is 100% community-funded. Across 2022 to 2025, 92% of funding came from private and community sources, with 74% from local businesses. 82% of every yen goes directly to trail construction; 95% to trails counting maintenance. Every trail has been fully sponsored.
