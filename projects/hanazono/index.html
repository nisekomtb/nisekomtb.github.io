Under Construction | Opening Summer 2026

Niseko's First Lift-Served Blue, Top to Bottom

NAMBA is building Hanazono's first mountain bike trails. Other resorts in Niseko have lift-served downhill, but only on advanced black trails or runs with black sections mixed in. Hanazono will be the first park where intermediate riders can ride lift to base, top to bottom.
Phase 1 opens this summer with a 4,200m blue flow trail running top to bottom, plus a skills park at the base. We're already planning what comes after.

Growth Timeline

2024
Masterplan pitched

In late 2024, NAMBA shows Hanazono the regional masterplan and makes the case that now is the time to invest in mountain biking and join the connected network.

2025
Permitting + first kilometre

Hanazono spans many landowners and forest types, so permitting takes most of the year. The first 1km of the flow trail is built by the end of the season.

2026
Phase 1 opens · 5.8km

The 4.2km lift-served flow trail and the 1.4km forest loop open as Phase 1 in summer 2026. Build continues after the opening: half of the next blue trail, more progression lines turning the forest loop into a proper skills centre, and a green loop trail at the top of the Hana1 lift. Planning for next year's expansion gets underway too.

Full build vision

NAMBA's Role

  • Advising on the regional masterplan
  • Consulting on trail design
  • Designing the unified signage system
  • Trail building

What's Being Built

  • 4,200m lift-served blue flow trail, top to bottom
  • 1,400m green forest loop for first-timers and families
  • Skills park at the base
  • Hiking trail network activated via the Hanazono 1 lift
  • More trails already planned for 2027 and beyond

Unified Signage

NAMBA designs Hanazono's trail signage using the same system we use at Twin Peaks and Grand Hirafu. Riders see the same signs everywhere we manage.

Be part of the build
