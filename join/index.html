Be Counted

Your membership is your vote for the future of mountain biking in Niseko. Every member strengthens NAMBA's voice when we work with resorts, government, and partners to build what comes next.

Why membership matters

Fund trails directly

92% of all funds go directly to trail building and maintenance. Your membership creates new trails.

Strengthen our voice

Numbers matter. When NAMBA sits down with resorts and government, membership count is the proof that the community wants this.

Path to nintei NPO

Growing membership brings NAMBA closer to nintei NPO status, which unlocks tax-deductible donations for all supporters.
