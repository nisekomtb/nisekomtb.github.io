How to add it to Gmail

  1. Pick your name above, then click Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (the gear icon, top right) and click See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new (or pick an existing one to edit).
  4. Click into the signature box and paste with Cmd/Ctrl + V. The card, photo, and logo should appear.
  5. Under the box, set the defaults so it is used For new emails and On reply/forward.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Images appear only after the site has deployed. If the photo or logo shows up blank, wait for the latest deploy and paste again.
