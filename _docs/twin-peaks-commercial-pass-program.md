# Twin Peaks Bike Park

## Commercial Pass Program

### Summer 2025

## Overview

The Twin Peaks Bike Park Commercial Pass Program aims to enhance the safety and quality of commercial guiding, instructing, and touring services within the Park. This program requires all commercial operators to obtain a pass, adhere to NAMBA's rules and standards and display proof of pass while conducting business. The program includes various safety, environmental, and operational guidelines to ensure a positive experience for all park users.

**Registration:** ¥300,000 JPY cooperation fee (exclusive of consumption tax) to register for the Twin Peaks Bike Park Commercial Pass Program. Registration includes 1 commercial operator permit for 1 guide.

**Additional passes:** ¥50,000 JPY cooperation fee (exclusive of consumption tax) to register for each additional pass (up to 2 additional permits). For more pricing information, see Article 5.

**Client Ratios:** Maximum of 1:6 guide to client ratio on all trails within the Park, with a maximum group size of 8. For more information, see Article 3.

## 1. Introduction

The Twin Peaks Bike Park (hereinafter referred to as "the Park") is a world-class mountain biking destination managed by the Niseko Area Mountain Bike Association (NAMBA). For the 2025 season, NAMBA is introducing a pilot program requiring all commercial operators, including but not limited to guides, instructors, and photo tour operators, to purchase an operating pass to conduct business within the Park.

### 1.1 Purpose

To regulate and elevate the quality of guiding and instructing services within the Park while minimizing the impact on non-commercial trail use.

### 1.2 Affected Parties

This program applies to businesses and individual operators conducting business within the park including but not limited to guiding or instruction businesses, operators, bike shops who charge riders to join them, classes, group rides, season-long classes / groups, kids groups, photo tours, guided hikes, and other commercial activities within the Park.

## 2. Definitions

**Commercial Users / Commercial Operations:** entities, but not limited to, individuals, companies, organizations, groups, etc., conducting services within the park for remuneration.

**Commercial Use:** Use where remuneration services is received.

## 3. Affiliation Requirements

To obtain a Commercial Pass, businesses, guides, and instructors must:

a. Abide by NAMBA's Code of Conduct at all times.

b. Provide documentation including proof of liability insurance, qualifications, first aid certification, and other documents as reasonably required by NAMBA.

c. Demonstrate adherence to environmental and safety standards, commitment to trail maintenance, and compliance with all relevant regulations.

## 4. Permit Limitations

### 4.1 Guide-to-client ratios

The pass allows for a maximum guide to client ratio of 1:6 within the Park.

### 4.2 Group size

The total group size must not exceed 8 individuals, including guides, clients, and any additional support personnel (e.g., tail guides, photographers). In accordance with Article 3.1, the maximum client-to-guide ratio of 1:6 must be maintained.

### 4.3 Operating Hours

- Members of the Twin Peaks Bike Park Commercial Pass Program must follow NAMBA's direction regarding closure of the Park, and may not operate outside of the Park's posted operating hours.
- NAMBA reserves the right to suspend commercial operations or close the Park at its discretion due to events, weather, or other reasons deemed appropriate and may do so without notice.
- Commercial guiding during Park closure is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Commercial Operator Permit Pricing

Seasonal Operating Permit: ¥300,000 cooperation for operation rights for the 2025 season, including one (1) commercial permit. Additional permits may be obtained through a ¥50,000 cooperation fee per additional permit (up to 2 extra permits).

## 6. Pass Transferability

A Commercial Operator Permit is valid for use by one guide/individual staff member at a time, but is transferable among guides affiliated with the same company.

## 7. Application Process

- **Initial Application:** Complete the Commercial Operator Permit Application form on NAMBA's website.
- **Submission of Documentation:** Submit necessary documentation including proof of business registration and certifications for guides and instructors.
- **Application Review:** NAMBA will review the documentation and assess adherence to safety and environmental standards.
- **On-site Evaluation:** NAMBA may conduct an on-site evaluation to assess operations and compliance.
- **Affiliation Approval:** Upon successful review, NAMBA will grant the entity a Commercial Operator Permit.
- **Annual Renewal:** Permits are subject to annual renewal, review and approval requiring ongoing compliance with NAMBA standards.

## 8. Prohibited Acts

The following acts are explicitly prohibited within the Park and may result in suspension or ban from the Park, and revocation of Commercial Operating Passes:

a. Unauthorized trail modifications.

b. Disruptive or unsafe behavior.

c. Environmental violations.

d. Non-compliance with regulations.

e. Operating during Park closure.

f. Unauthorized events.

g. Transacting business within the Park.

h. Advertising within the Park.

i. Any behavior deemed against NAMBA's ideals.

j. Removal of flora or fauna from the Park.

k. Combining multiple groups, or riding in groups larger than permitted by the guide-to-client ratios prescribed in Article 3 of this document.

## 9. Enforcement Procedures

### 9.1 Warning System

Verbal warning for the first offense, written warning for the second, and temporary or permanent ban for the third.

### 9.2 Suspension, Revocation and Bans

NAMBA may suspend or revoke permits, or ban an organization, for repeated or severe violations.

### 9.3 Ban Period and Restrictions

Bans are served only when the Park is open and may carry over to the next season if imposed during closure periods.

NAMBA reserves the right to ban individual staff members, or entire companies, at its sole discretion for non-compliance with the rules herein or NAMBA's Code of Conduct.

## 10. Display of Commercial Pass

Commercial operators are required to display their proof of commercial pass in a manner that is clearly visible at all times while conducting business within the Park.

## 11. Trail Etiquette

Holding a Commercial Pass does not grant ownership of the trails – be mindful of other trail users. Passholders and their clients must follow trail etiquette best practices including:

a. Stepping off the trail when stopped to avoid blocking the path.

b. Yielding to other users when safe to do so.

c. Allowing safe passing for faster riders or other trail users.

d. Maintaining control and being aware of your surroundings at all times.

e. Following all posted signs, trail markers and instructions from NAMBA and its staff.

f. Refrain from "convoy guiding," which involves multiple groups riding in rapid succession – either within your own organization or with other guiding organizations. Guides engaging in "convoy guiding" may be deemed a violation of Article 8 "Prohibited Acts", Item i.

## 12. Photo and Portrait Rights

By participating in activities within the Park, guides, instructors, and their clients grant NAMBA the right to use any photos or videos taken of them for promotional purposes without compensation.

## 13. Safety and Emergency Procedures

Guides and instructors, and their clients, must familiarize themselves with and follow Twin Peaks' rules and safety procedures.

Guides and instructors are required to develop, and provide for review, their own emergency response documents, including procedures for handling injuries, missing persons, fire, natural disasters, wildlife encounters, and other emergencies.

## 14. Liability and Indemnity

### 14.1 Insurance Requirement

Guiding and instructor businesses must carry comprehensive liability insurance.

### 14.2 Assumption of Risk

All guides, instructors, and their clients acknowledge that mountain biking and related activities involve inherent risks, including but not limited to serious injury or death. By participating in activities within the Park, they assume all risks associated with these activities.

### 14.3 Indemnity for NAMBA

To the fullest extent permitted by law, all commercial operators, guides, instructors, and their clients shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless NAMBA, its officers, directors, employees, agents, and volunteers from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, and expenses (including but not limited to attorneys' fees) arising out of or in any way connected with their use of the Park, including but not limited to claims arising from the negligence of the indemnified parties.

### 14.4 No Guarantee of Maintenance Quality

NAMBA makes no warranty, express or implied, regarding a standard of condition, quality, or maintenance of the Park trails and facilities. All users acknowledge that the Park trails and facilities are used at their own risk, and NAMBA shall not be held liable for any injuries, damages, or losses incurred.

## 15. Affiliation Benefits

Membership in the Commercial Pass Program secures the Pass-holding business various benefits, including:

a. **Logo Placement:** Affiliated companies' logos will be displayed on NAMBA's website.

b. **Exclusive Operating Rights:** Only Commercial Pass holders are allowed to operate within the Park.

### 15.1 Disclaimer of Non-Partnership

The purchase of a commercial pass does not establish NAMBA as a partner, joint venture, employer, or agent of any business.

## 16. General Information

### 16.1 Identification Requirement

Pass holders must display their pass and identify themselves to staff and other trail users if requested.

### 16.2 Exclusivity

Commercial passes do not grant exclusive use of space but provide a license for activity in the Park.

### 16.3 Exclusions

The following are not required to pay a cooperation fee for a commercial pass:

a. Non-commercial use.

b. Unpaid group rides, including those organized by clubs or shops. Such rides must be free to attend and open to the general public, and must endeavor to adhere to the principles of this document.

c. General public users of the Park.

### 16.4 Disclaimer of Maintenance and Quality

NAMBA does not guarantee the quality of trail maintenance. All operators and their customers/clients use the Park and its trails entirely at their own risk.

## Appendix: NAMBA Code of Conduct

(https://namba.ngo/code-conduct/)

NAMBA trail users can help maintain trails and healthy mountain biking culture by following these guidelines.

### Respect the environment

- Do not ride on trails where mountain biking is prohibited and/or signage to this effect is present.
- Do not construct unauthorized trails.
- Riders are to stay on the trails.
- Always enter a trail area through an authorized entry point.
- Stay off muddy trails after rain.
- Carry out what you carry in – Leave No Trace.
- Keep your bike clean, and avoid spreading weeds.
- Don't move rocks, logs or change the trail without permission.
- Control your bike and stay on the trail.
- Ride in a manner not to cause damage to the trail.
- Do not ride on trails closed for maintenance.

### Respect Others

- Respect other trail users.
- Always expect someone may be around the next corner.
- Ring a bell and/or announce your presence to other trail users.
- Slow down and give way when approaching others.
- Always pass with care.
- Ride in small groups where possible.
- Control your speed.

### Respect yourself

- Always wear a helmet and protective gear.
- Ride within your ability.
- Expect foreseeable risks.
- Plan your ride and pack accordingly.

**Be a steward.** We are all representatives of the mountain bike community. If you meet other trail users who are not following this code, politely explain to them that only by keeping to its guidelines can we ensure that mountain bike riding remains an accepted recreational pursuit in forest areas and on public land.

Lastly, volunteer your time to helping with trail maintenance, running of events, and participating in activities by being an active member of our community.
