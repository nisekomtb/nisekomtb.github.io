  • Your logo on large trail map boards at key intersections
  • Signage photos regularly featured in media coverage (Cyclowired, etc.)

Industry Partners

For mountain bike and outdoor industry brands, we offer partnership on a product basis. Provide equipment, apparel, or services in exchange for the same exposure benefits as our community partners. Brands like Specialized, Peatys and Smith have already joined the network.

  • Same exposure benefits as community partners
  • Product contribution instead of (or alongside) cash
  • Co-branded event opportunities
  • Demo and testing opportunities at NAMBA events

Trail Sponsorship

Fund the building of a trail and your brand becomes permanently part of the landscape. Trail sponsors receive all Higuma-level benefits plus extensive signage exposure: your logo on the trail's sign, on map boards, and on trail markers at least every 500m. Trail sponsorship also includes a lifetime commercial operator pass.

2026 Trail Opportunities

Lower Green Climb

In construction 2,000m · ¥7,000,000

Climb trails see slower traffic, giving sponsor signage longer dwell time and stronger brand impressions. Lower Green Climb opens easier access to the west side of the mountain, making it a popular route not just for beginners, but for anyone who would rather not ride all the way to the top.

Green Flow Trail

In planning 1,500m · ¥11,000,000

Niseko's first proper beginner downhill course. The area has plenty of skills parks and easy loops, but Green Flow bridges the gap between skills park and intermediate terrain, a real downhill trail tailored to beginners. Build planned for 2026.
