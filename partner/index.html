Your Brand in the Park

Partner logos are displayed on individual trail signs, large trail map boards, and trailhead signage. All signage is strategically placed for high visibility, seen by every visitor to the park.

Photo: Trail marker sign showing sponsor logo and trail information

  • Your logo on individual trail signs, seen by every rider
  • Your logo on large trail map boards at key intersections
  • Signage photos regularly featured in media coverage (Cyclowired, etc.)
  • As the network expands to Grand Hirafu and Hanazono, signage exposure grows with it

Industry Partners

For mountain bike and outdoor industry brands, we offer partnership on a product basis. Provide equipment, apparel, or services in exchange for the same exposure benefits as our community partners. Brands like Peatys and Smith have already joined the network.

  • Same exposure benefits as community partners
  • Product contribution instead of (or alongside) cash
  • Co-branded event opportunities
  • Demo and testing opportunities at NAMBA events

Trail Sponsorship

Fund the building of a trail and your brand becomes permanently part of the landscape. Trail sponsors receive all Higuma-level benefits plus extensive signage exposure: your logo on the trail's sign, on map boards, and on trail markers at least every 500m. Trail sponsorship also includes a lifetime commercial operator pass.
