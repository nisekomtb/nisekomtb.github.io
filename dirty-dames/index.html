Dirty Dames is NAMBA's women's mountain biking programme in the Niseko area. Since launching in 2024, over 200 women have joined our rides, skills sessions, and community events.

What is Dirty Dames

A place for women to ride together, pick up skills, and build confidence on the trails. Whether it's your first time on a mountain bike or you want a faster group, Dirty Dames is for you.

  • Group rides

    Throughout the season, June to October.

  • Skills sessions

    For beginners and intermediate riders.

  • Social events

    Community building and connection off the bike.

  • Open to all

    All women, all abilities, all ages.

  • Free to join

    No membership or registration fee required.

Why it matters

Mountain biking has historically been male-dominated, especially in Japan. Dirty Dames is changing that in Niseko, with a programme built for women across all abilities and ages.

  • 200+ riders

    Have joined since the programme launched in 2024.

  • A full programme

    Group rides, skills sessions, and socials every season.

  • Built for inclusion

    Part of NAMBA's commitment to making the sport welcoming for everyone.
