Dirty Dames

Dirty Dames is NAMBA's women's mountain biking programme, created to grow and support women's participation in the sport across the Niseko area. Since launching in 2024, over 200 women have joined rides, skills sessions, and community events.

Photo: Group of women at a Dirty Dames ride or event, smiling, diverse, welcoming atmosphere

What is Dirty Dames

A welcoming space for women of all abilities to ride together, learn new skills, and build confidence on the trails. Whether you've never been on a mountain bike or you're looking for a group to push your riding, Dirty Dames is for you.
  • Group rides: Throughout the season (June to October)
  • Skills sessions: For beginners and intermediate riders
  • Social events: Community building and connection
  • Open to all: All women, all abilities, all ages
  • Free to join: No membership or registration fee required

Why it matters

Mountain biking has historically been male-dominated. Dirty Dames exists to change that in Niseko. By creating dedicated programming for women, NAMBA is building a more inclusive riding community and helping grow the sport for everyone.
  • 200+ participants since the 2024 launch
  • Multiple rides and events per season
  • Part of NAMBA's commitment to accessibility and inclusion
