Twin Peaks Bike Park has its own beer. The Twin Peaks Bike Park Pale Ale is a Niseko craft beer brewed by Yotei Brewing, with label art by Joe Scott and a donation to the trails built into every can.

A beer for the bike park

Yotei Brewing has backed NAMBA as a sponsor for years, and their brewery sits in Niseko Town, a short ride from the park. When the idea of a collaboration beer came up, neither side needed much convincing. The base is A Greener World, Yotei's pale ale brewed partly with green energy, wearing a new label drawn for the park.

Niseko's mountain bike scene is growing fast, and Swiss-designed trails only get built when local businesses back them. Yotei Brewing already supports NAMBA, so a beer together was an easy yes, and Joe Scott's label makes it unmistakably Niseko. Every can sold goes straight back into the dirt, and into whatever we build next.

Tom Mortiboy, Co-founder and Head of Marketing & Communication, NAMBA

The label

Joe Scott drew the label: the Twin Peaks entrance gate with Mount Yotei rising behind it, in the national park poster style he works in. Both are real. The gate stands at the trailhead above Hirafu, and the mountain needs no introduction. Joe also designed the Twin Peaks badge in our Artist Series, where you can see more of his work.

The beer itself

  • Style: Pale Ale
  • ABV: 4.5%
  • IBU: 35
  • Hops: Amarillo, Mosaic, Simcoe
  • Volume: 350ml can
  • Base beer: A Greener World
  • Brewed by: Yotei Brewing, Niseko
  • Label art: Joe Scott

It's sold through the Yotei Brewing online shop.

Where the money goes

A portion of every sale is donated to NAMBA. That money buys trail work: drainage, resurfacing, new features, and the unglamorous digging that keeps 17km+ of singletrack running. Maintenance cost ¥6.5 million in 2024 and ¥10.8 million in 2025, and it climbs as the network grows. Twin Peaks is free to ride, and local businesses like Yotei are a big part of why it can stay that way. The full numbers are on our impact page.
