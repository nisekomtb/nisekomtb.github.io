<h3 id="join-our-volunteer-subcommittee-teams">Join our volunteer subcommittee teams!</h3>

Volunteers are the backbone of NAMBA: our arms, legs, brain, and heart. Every subcommittee is volunteer-run.

<strong>NAMBA is growing, and we need more volunteers to keep up.</strong> Right now we especially need help on the Access and Admin subcommittees.

Join one of the following subcommittees:

- <h4>Trails Subcommittee:</h4>
  <div class="desc">Plans, designs, builds, and maintains NAMBA's trails. Works closely with the Access Subcommittee on permitting and landowner relationships.</div>

- <h4>Access Subcommittee:</h4>
  <div class="desc">Builds and maintains relationships with local landowners and stakeholders, both private and public. Without access, there are no trails. Works with the Trails Subcommittee on permits, design, and compliance.</div>

- <h4>Events Subcommittee:</h4>
  <div class="desc">Plans and runs NAMBA events: group rides, community races, fundraisers, and demo days. Works with the Comms &amp; Marketing Subcommittee and external sponsors.</div>

- <h4>Communications &amp; Marketing Subcommittee:</h4>
  <div class="desc">Runs the NAMBA brand: marketing content, fundraisers, the website, and Trailforks integration.</div>

- <h4>Admin Subcommittee:</h4>
  <div class="desc">Keeps NAMBA running behind the scenes: accounting, grant applications, and administrative processes.</div>
