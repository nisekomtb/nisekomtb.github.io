## Join our volunteer subcommittee teams!

Volunteers are the backbone of NAMBA: our arms, legs, brain, and heart. Every subcommittee is volunteer-run.

<strong>NAMBA is growing, and we need more volunteers to keep up.</strong> Right now we especially need help on the Access and Admin subcommittees.

Join one of the following subcommittees:

- ### Trails Subcommittee:
  Plans, designs, builds, and maintains NAMBA's trails. Works closely with the Access Subcommittee on permitting and landowner relationships.
- ### Access Subcommittee:
  Builds and maintains relationships with local landowners and stakeholders, both private and public. Without access, there are no trails. Works with the Trails Subcommittee on permits, design, and compliance.
- ### Events Subcommittee:
  Plans and runs NAMBA events: group rides, community races, fundraisers, and demo days. Works with the Comms &amp; Marketing Subcommittee and external sponsors.
- ### Communications &amp; Marketing Subcommittee:
  Runs the NAMBA brand: marketing content, fundraisers, the website, and Trailforks integration.
- ### Admin Subcommittee:
  Keeps NAMBA running behind the scenes: accounting, grant applications, and administrative processes.
