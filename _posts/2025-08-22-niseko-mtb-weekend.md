Niseko Mountain Bike Weekend, 4-day MTB celebration! Come demo Santa Cruz bikes, get tips from Pro riders and enjoy Niseko Area MTB trails!

Special guest riders: <a href="https:instagram.com/hajimeeeee" target="blank">Hajime Imoto</a> and <a href="https:instagram.com/yukikushima" target="blank">Yuki Kushima</a> from Santa Cruz Bicycles.

### Event Schedule:

- <strong>August 22 (am):</strong> Ladies Ride Session @ Twin Peaks

- <strong>August 22 (pm):</strong> Kids Session @ Twin Peaks

- <strong>August 23–24:</strong> Downhill Series @ Niseko Annupuri

- <strong>August 25:</strong> Free Ride Session

### Rides:

Ladies & Kids ride will be led by one of the pro riders. All levels welcome, kids need to be able to ride a bike.

Meeting point: Rhythm Niseko, upstairs on the bridge.
