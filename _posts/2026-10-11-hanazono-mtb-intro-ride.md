Always wanted to try mountain biking? Niseko Hanazono Resort is running free
beginner sessions over its WEEKEND ESCAPE weekend, and on the Monday our own Angharad
and Dan are the ones teaching. Kids and adults are both welcome, and you don't
need to have ridden off-road before.

## What you'll learn

Each session lasts about an hour and a half. You'll start with the basics of
how a mountain bike works, then braking and body position, and finish with a
short ride on the Mori Loop through the autumn forest.

## Bring a bike, or rent one

Both mountain bikes and cross bikes are fine. If you don't have one, Niseko
Sports at the resort rents bikes, paid separately.

## Turning up

There's nothing to book. Meet at Hanazono EDGE a few minutes before 10:00am or
2:00pm on whichever day suits you. If you have questions, call Hanazono on
+81 136-21-6688 or email bookings@harmonyresorts.com.

Enjoyed it? Hanazono has [plenty more trails](/where-to-ride/hanazono/) to work
up to, and [Twin Peaks](/twin-peaks/) is free to ride until the end of October.
