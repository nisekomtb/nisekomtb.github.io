The autumn ride is the season closer for the Niseko cycling community. Each September the forest tracks dry out and riders gather under Mt Yotei for two days on the gravel roads and farm tracks that link the villages around town.

## What you need to know

- **Date:** Saturday 26 and Sunday 27 September 2026
- **Venue:** Niseko Central Warehouse Complex, in central Niseko village
- **Format:** Self-guided Sunday rally, three course options, checkpoint and aid-station based, not a race
- **Who it's for:** Riders comfortable on gravel and mixed surfaces. Three distances, from 50 km social pace to 120 km endurance.

NAMBA is a community sponsor of the ride and you will see us at the start and finish. Niseko Gravel runs the show; head over to their site for the full course brief and to register.
