**Nakama** (仲間) is Japanese for your crew, the people you ride with. That's what this ride is about: turning up, meeting other riders, and sharing a few laps at [Twin Peaks](/twin-peaks/) as the day winds down.

The hills are starting to turn. Crisp air, golden light, and some of the best riding conditions of the year.

We meet at the Twin Peaks base at **4:00pm on the last Monday of the month**. Dan from our trail crew leads the way, and we're out for one to two hours at an easy, social pace. No racing, nobody dropped, everyone rolls back together.

Intermediate skill level or above is recommended, but not required. If you're comfortable getting down a blue trail, you'll fit right in. Come with a mate, or turn up on your own and leave with a few new ones.

## The details

- **When:** Monday 28 September 2026, from 4:00pm (one to two hours)
- **Where:** Meet at the [Twin Peaks base](https://goo.gl/maps/yKza3NA7yfx5VQRx8)
- **Pace:** Easy and social, led by Dan from the NAMBA trail crew
- **Ability:** Intermediate or above recommended, but all abilities welcome
- **Cost:** Free, just turn up
- **Weather:** Wet weather cancels the ride. If the forecast looks grim, check [@nisekomtb](https://www.instagram.com/nisekomtb/) for the call

New to Twin Peaks? Come a little early to find your bearings and take a look at [the trail map](/twin-peaks/) before we head out. Can't make this one? There's one more Nakama Ride to come in October, plus everything else on our [events page](/events/).
