Bikes take a beating on the Niseko trails, and a bit of regular care keeps them rolling well and out of the repair queue. Rhythm Hirafu is running a free evening clinic at their workshop, covering the basics of bike care and a simple tune-up. It is a relaxed, hands-on session for anyone who rides around Hirafu and wants to learn how to look after their own bike.

## What you need to know

- **Date:** Friday 17 July 2026, from 6:00pm
- **Venue:** Rhythm Hirafu, a short walk from the Grand Hirafu welcome centre and gondola
- **Cost:** Free. Space is limited to the first 20 sign-ups
- **Language:** English instruction only
- **Bring:** Just yourself. Please do not bring your own bike; Rhythm has everything you need on the night

Rhythm runs the clinic and places are capped at 20, so book early. A bit of bike know-how goes a long way before your next lap on [Twin Peaks](/twin-peaks/) or a day at the [Hanazono bike park](/where-to-ride/hanazono/).
