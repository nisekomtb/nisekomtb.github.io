NAMBA is building Japan's largest free-access mountain bike trail network in Niseko, Hokkaido. Below you'll find key facts, story angles, and downloadable assets for media coverage.

Key Facts

  • 21km+ of free, professionally designed mountain bike trails
  • 11,500+ visitors in 2025 (~48% growth year-on-year)
  • 20,000+ volunteer hours donated by the community
  • 100% trail funding success rate, every trail community-funded
  • Swiss-designed trails in partnership with Allegra Tourismus
  • Multi-park network: Twin Peaks + Grand Hirafu + Hanazono (opening July 2026)
  • Pinkbike Trail of the Month feature confirmed
  • Founded in 2021 by 6 mountain bikers, now a 24-person board
  • Japan's only truly open-access, nonprofit-built trail network. No membership, no registration, no fees

Story Angles

The Grassroots Story

How six friends with no trails built Japan's largest free-access MTB network in under five years, entirely through community support.

The Destination Story

What snowsports did for Niseko's winters, mountain biking is doing for its summers. From one park to an interconnected network spanning three resorts.

The Japan Story

The only truly open-access, nonprofit-built trail network in Japan. No membership, no registration, no fees. Just show up and ride.
