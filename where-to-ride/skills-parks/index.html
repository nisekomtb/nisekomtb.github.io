Skills Parks & Pump Tracks

Whether you're a beginner building confidence, a parent introducing kids to riding, or an experienced rider sharpening skills, Niseko's skills parks and pump tracks are for everyone.

Twin Peaks Skills Centre

  • Location: At the base of Twin Peaks Bike Park
  • Cost: Free
  • What's there: A skills area focused on learning to ride trails, with wooden features added during the 2025 Trail Days event. Includes a balance bike loop for the youngest riders.
  • Suitability: All ages and levels. The balance bike loop is perfect for children as young as 2.

Tomo Playpark Pump Track

  • Location: Akaigawa village (approximately 30 minutes drive from Niseko)
  • Cost: Adults ¥1,200 (2 hours) / ¥1,600 (full day), elementary ¥900 / ¥1,200, helmet rental ¥500
  • What's there: A proper Velosolutions pump track with a long course. Suitable for BMX, dirt jumpers, MTB, and skateboarders. Camping facilities available on site.
  • Suitability: Ages 7+. Helmet mandatory (rental available). Bring your own bike.
  • Season: May to October
  • More info: playpark.akaigawa-tomo.com

Grand Hirafu Skill-up Area

  • Location: Grand Hirafu base area
  • Cost: Free
  • What's there: A practice course for beginners and intermediate riders, with Mt Yotei views. Staff support available for those new to the sport.
  • Suitability: Children and first-timers welcome
  • More info: grand-hirafu.jp

Rusutsu Pump Track

  • Location: Rusutsu (approximately 30 minutes drive from Niseko)
  • What's there: A short flow trail in pump track style. Perfect for beginners and first-timers.
  • Suitability: Beginners and first-timers
  • More info: Trailforks

Rhythm Japan Skills Park

  • Location: Rhythm Japan main store, Niseko Hirafu
  • Cost: Free
  • What's there: Currently a modular pump track with free usage. A full skills park is being installed.
  • Suitability: All levels
