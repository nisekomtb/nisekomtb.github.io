Skills Parks & Pump Tracks

Whether you're a beginner building confidence, a parent introducing kids to riding, or an experienced rider sharpening skills — Niseko's skills parks and pump tracks are for everyone.

Facilities

Twin Peaks Skills Centre

  • Location — At the base of Twin Peaks Bike Park.
  • What's there — Skills features, balance bike loop for the youngest riders, and a great starting point before heading out on the trails.
  • Suitability — All ages and levels. The balance bike loop is perfect for children as young as 2.

Hanazono Skills Park

Opening July 2026
  • Location — At the base of the Hanazono 1 lift.
  • What's there — A purpose-built skills park designed as part of the new Hanazono trail project. Details to be announced closer to opening.
  • Suitability — Designed for all levels, with a focus on making mountain biking accessible to beginners.

Akaigawa Pump Track

  • Location — In nearby Akaigawa village, a short drive from the main Niseko area.
  • What's there — A pump track in a scenic rural setting. NAMBA has hosted community events here.
  • Suitability — Fun for all levels. A great side trip for families or riders looking for something different.
