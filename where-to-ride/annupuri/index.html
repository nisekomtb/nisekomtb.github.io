Niseko's natural-line downhill

Niseko Annupuri runs a single downhill course, served by the Annupuri gondola from summit to base. The course design was supervised by professional MTB athlete Kazuki Shimizu, and it currently hosts the Downhill Series Niseko round each summer.

The trail is raw and natural: fast, loose, and off-camber, built with minimal slope modification. It isn't recommended for beginners.

Hotels, restaurants, and onsen sit at the base of the gondola. Annupuri isn't part of the NAMBA network, so the unified trail signage from the other parks doesn't carry over here.
