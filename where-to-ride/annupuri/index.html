Gondola-Accessed Downhill

Niseko Annupuri offers a full-scale downhill course supervised by professional MTB athlete Kazuki Shimizu. The 2,750m course drops 535 metres from summit to base via the Annupuri gondola. Fast, raw, and built with minimal slope modification for a natural riding experience.

The course

Four sections spanning 2,750m with a total elevation drop of 535m (935m to 400m altitude). The terrain is maintained naturally with minimal modification, creating a rough-and-ready, off-camber riding experience.
  • A1: 640m, 140m drop
  • B1: 600m, 130m drop
  • C1: 600m, 100m drop
  • D1: 910m, 165m drop

Before you ride

  • Difficulty: Intermediate to advanced riders only
  • Lift access: Gondola to 935m altitude, approximately 10 minutes. Remove front wheel before boarding.
  • Season: Mid-July to mid-October (check Annupuri's website for exact dates)
  • Hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM (last gondola ascent)
  • Equipment: Full-face helmet mandatory, body protection strongly recommended. Downhill-capable bike with functioning brakes essential.
  • Rules: Downhill riding only, climbing prohibited. Clean mud from your bike before boarding the gondola.

Pricing
