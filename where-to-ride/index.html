Riding Areas

Twin Peaks Bike Park

{% include trail-difficulty-counts.html data_key="twinpeaks" %}

Japan's largest free-access MTB network with 20+ Swiss-designed trails across 21km+. Beginner flow to expert tech. Free, every day, no registration.

Explore Twin Peaks

Grand Hirafu

{% include trail-difficulty-counts.html data_key="grand-hirafu" %}

Bike park expansion with lift access in Niseko's largest resort village. Trail building and consulting by NAMBA.

Hanazono

{% include trail-difficulty-counts.html data_key="hanazono" %}

Niseko's first lift-accessed top-to-bottom blue trail featuring a 4,500m flow trail plus skills park. Opening Summer 2026.

Niseko Annupuri

{% include trail-difficulty-counts.html data_key="annupuri" %}

Gondola-accessed downhill course. 2,750m of natural terrain with 535m of elevation drop. Intermediate to advanced riders.
