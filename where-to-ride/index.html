Riding Areas

Twin Peaks Bike Park

Japan's largest free-access MTB network — 20+ Swiss-designed trails across 21km+. Beginner flow to expert tech. Free, every day, no registration.

Bike park expansion with lift access in Niseko's largest resort village. Trail building and consulting by NAMBA.
