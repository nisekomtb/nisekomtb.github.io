Niseko's Largest Resort Village

Grand Hirafu, Niseko's largest resort village, offers lift-accessed mountain biking as part of the growing Niseko trail network. NAMBA consults on trail design and builds trails at Hirafu, ensuring the riding experience connects seamlessly with Twin Peaks and the wider network.

What's available

  • Trails: Multiple trails across varying difficulty levels, with more being added each season.
  • Lift access: Gondola and chair lift access during the summer operating period.
  • Difficulty range: Options from intermediate to advanced, complementing Twin Peaks' trail network.

Getting there

  • Location: In the heart of Hirafu village, the main hub of the Niseko resort area. A short drive from Twin Peaks and Hanazono.
  • Parking: Free parking available at the base area during summer.
  • Bike rentals: Rental shops are available in Hirafu village. See our trip planning guide for details.

Connected to Twin Peaks

Grand Hirafu's trails connect to the wider Niseko network, including Twin Peaks Bike Park. As the network grows, riders can move between areas for a full day of varied riding across multiple parks.

More information

For full trail details, conditions, and lift pass information, visit the Grand Hirafu resort website.
