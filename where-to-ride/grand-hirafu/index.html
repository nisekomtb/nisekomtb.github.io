Niseko's original bike park

Grand Hirafu is Niseko's original mountain bike park, now served top to bottom by the new Ace Gondola. Fast laps from summit to base with Mount Yotei across the valley.

The middle-section Kuro Obi trail carries significant exposure and isn't recommended for beginners or riders new to lift-served descents.

The bike park sits in the heart of Hirafu Village, with accommodation, food, and onsen on the doorstep. Twin Peaks Bike Park is a short ride away.
