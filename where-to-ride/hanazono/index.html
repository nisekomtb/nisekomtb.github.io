Opening July 2026

Niseko's First Lift-Accessed Riding

Hanazono brings something entirely new to Niseko — lift-accessed riding. A 4,500m flow trail descending top-to-bottom via the Hanazono 1 lift makes this Niseko's first lift-accessed blue trail. Combined with a skills park at the base and a 1,500m link trail connecting to Twin Peaks, Hanazono opens Niseko to a whole new style of riding.

What to expect

  • Flow trail — A 4,500m blue-graded flow trail from the top of the Hanazono 1 lift to the base. Flowing berms, rollers, and gentle gradients designed for maximum fun.
  • Skills park — A purpose-built skills area at the base of the lift, perfect for warming up or building confidence before heading to the trail.
  • Lift access — Ride the Hanazono 1 lift with your bike and descend the flow trail — no pedalling uphill required.
  • Twin Peaks connection — A 1,500m link trail connects Hanazono to the Twin Peaks network, making it possible to ride between both areas in a single session.

For all levels

Hanazono's terrain complements Twin Peaks' intermediate and advanced focus — the blue flow trail is designed for riders of all abilities, making Niseko accessible to true beginners for the first time with lift access.
Whether you're a first-time mountain biker, a family looking for a safe and fun ride, or an experienced rider wanting effortless laps — Hanazono delivers.
