Opening Summer 2026

Niseko's newest bike park

Niseko Hanazono Bike Park opens summer 2026 with two trails: a 4,200m blue intermediate descending from the top of the Hanazono 1 lift, and a 1,360m green beginner loop accessed by a short pedal climb.

The trails

  • Forest Loop (green, 1,360m). A short, easy pedal climb to a beginner-friendly loop. Designed for first-timers and families.
  • Downhill Course (blue, 4,200m). A flow-style blue intermediate descent from the top of the Hanazono 1 lift to the base. Top-to-bottom on the lift, no pedalling required.
