Opening Summer 2026

Niseko's newest bike park

Niseko Hanazono Bike Park opens summer 2026 with two new trails, headlined by Niseko's first lift-accessed top-to-bottom intermediate descent. The 4,200m Downhill Course drops from the top of the Hanazono 1 lift to the base, a flow-style blue with no pedalling required.

The 1,360m Forest Loop offers a beginner-friendly green ride reached by a short, easy pedal climb, built for first-timers and families.
