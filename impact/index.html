Funding Transparency

Where It Comes From, Where It Goes

Local Partnerships
Funding from our major resort partners (Grand Hirafu, Hanazono) and local Niseko businesses. These partners recognise the value of trails for the year-round community.
Industry Sponsors
Direct support from global mountain bike brands (e.g. Santa Cruz Pay Dirt, Winckler, Specialized). The global bike industry's investment in Niseko.
Grants & Subsidies
Competitive funding secured from government bodies and tourism associations (e.g. JTB, NPB, KTA) to support regional development.
Community Fund
"Rider-powered" income. Every T-shirt and cap sold, individual donations, and local raffle tickets.
Trail Construction
The heavy lifting. Payments to world-class trail contractors (Allegra), heavy machinery rentals, and the specialised logistics required to build new terrain.
Trail Maintenance & Operation
Keeping trails open, safe, and world-class. Our professional trail crew's wages, land access leases (like Twin Peaks), fuel, insurance, and essential tools.
Marketing & Events
Building the Niseko riding community. Volunteer dig days, community BBQs, trail signage, and social media to keep everyone informed and engaged.
Admin & Other
The "nuts and bolts" that keep the association legal and stable: professional accounting, bank fees, and required taxes (JCT).

Our Journey

From six riders with shovels to Asia's premier mountain bike network.

2021
Foundation

Six mountain bikers, saddened by the lack of mountain biking in Niseko, start meeting regularly. An NPO, they realise, is the way to have a voice.

2022
4.4 km · NPO formed

NPO officially registered. 17 board members join the founders, bringing the board to 23. Kutchan town approves trail-building rights at Twin Peaks. 4.4km built in a COVID-shortened window, under a 1m width restriction and without cutting down a single tree.

2023
Twin Peaks opens · ¥36M raised

Twin Peaks opens to the public. 6km built in one summer, the fastest project Allegra had ever seen. Network reaches 8.5km. ¥36.4M raised from sponsors. Hokkaido's first Soil Searching event.

2024
7,750 visitors · ¥91M raised

+7km of new trail across two resorts: 3.6km at Grand Hirafu, 3.4km at Twin Peaks (including our first double-black, Taki Tech). 7,750 visitors ride Twin Peaks. Dirty Dames launches with 30+ women at the opening ride. ¥90.9M raised, up 150% on 2023.

2025
11,500 visitors · ¥97M raised

Visitors up 48% to 11,500. Twin Peaks' first jump line opens, paired with a dedicated climb trail for hot laps. First 1.5km goes in at Hanazono. ¥97.3M raised in a single year.

2026
Hanazono opens · 31 km network

Hanazono opens in July with 5.6km of new trail. Grand Hirafu activates the new Ace Gondola for bikes, with 2km of new singletrack from the top. NAMBA-built trail crosses 23km, with the total Niseko network reaching 31km.
